    How to play

    A random character from the Cosmere has been chosen. Guess anyone, and each column tells you how close you are. Play as many rounds as you like.

    • Match. That attribute is exactly right.
    • Close. Some overlap: a shared name or ability, the same series but a different book, or the same species but a different subtype.
    • Miss. Nothing in common.

    Tip: in Settings you can pick which series the mystery character comes from, so you avoid spoilers for books you haven't read.

    Settings

    Mystery character comes from

    You can still guess any character. This only limits who the answer can be. If the current character is from a series you turn off, you get a new one when you close Settings.
